XDS Integration Scale

The per-image scale factor and spot statistics that XDS INTEGRATE writes to INTEGRATE.LP, plotted against image number or rotation angle. Load several files to compare datasets on the same axes.

Datasets

Files are named by the folder they sit in (exp12/INTEGRATE.LP becomes exp12), so loading a folder of experiments gives one curve each. Files can also be dropped onto this page. The rotation angle is the middle of each image: STARTING_ANGLE + (image − STARTING_FRAME + ½) × OSCILLATION_RANGE.

Scale factor

SCALE is the factor XDS applies to every reflection on that image. A smooth drift usually follows beam current, crystal volume in the beam or absorption along the rotation; single images that jump away from their neighbours are worth checking for a beam blank or a bad frame. Images with a non-zero error code (IER) are drawn hollow and left out of the statistics.

Spot statistics

same horizontal axis

Reflections near the Ewald sphere, strong spots used to learn the profiles, unexpected reflections, and the refined beam divergence (σB) and reflecting range (σR, mosaicity) for each image.
