Intensity Decay

Total integrated intensity of each dataset in acquisition order, with a fitted decay curve. When the falling intensity comes from a drop in electron flux, the fit also gives a correction factor that puts every dataset back on the scale of the first one.

Two columns, tab, comma or space separated, header optional: a dataset name and its total intensity, e.g. experiment_12  22225658. The number in the name sets the position on the x axis; without one the row order is used. Click a point to leave it out of the fit (it stays on the plot, hollow). The file can also be dropped onto this page.

Intensity against experiment

Correction factors

fit(first) / fit(n)

Multiplying a dataset’s intensities by its factor removes the fitted decay and puts it on the scale of the first experiment. Taken from the fitted curve rather than the raw sums, so the scatter of single datasets is not copied into the correction.
